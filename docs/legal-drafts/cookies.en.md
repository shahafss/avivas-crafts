# Cookie Policy

**Draft — requires legal review.** This text is not final and has not yet been checked by a professional.

## What the site stores on your device

The site stores on your device only what it needs in order to work: the cart, the language you chose, and the details you entered at checkout while you go to the payment page and back. These are strictly necessary items, so they do not need consent. The table below this text lists each item, what it is for and how long it stays. The table is generated from one list that the site itself uses, so it is always up to date.

The site stores nothing on your device that is not in the table.

## Statistics without tracking

We count how many people visited each page and which site they came from, with no cookies, no IP address and no identifier. It is therefore impossible to identify or follow you, and the count does not need consent.

## If optional cookies are ever turned on

Today the site uses no optional cookies (such as advertising or advanced analytics). If we ever turn some on:

- you will see a banner with the buttons "Accept all" and "Reject all", both equally prominent;
- nothing optional will load before you accept;
- you can choose by category and change your choice at any time through "Cookie settings" at the bottom of the site;
- your choice is saved on your device, and the database keeps only a record of the choice with no personal identifier, for two years;
- when this Cookie Policy changes we will ask you to choose again, and until then nothing optional loads.

For details about your personal data see the [Privacy Policy](/en/pages/privacy).
