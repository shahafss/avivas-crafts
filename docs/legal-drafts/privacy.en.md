# Privacy Policy

**Draft — requires legal review.** This text is not final and has not yet been checked by a professional.

## Who is responsible for your data

The owner of the business is the database controller and is responsible for the data collected on this site. The business name, address and contact details are shown at the bottom of every page and on the [Contact](/en/contact) page, and the business number is given in the order emails. For any privacy question, contact us through the contact page or at the email shown at the bottom of the site.

## What data we collect and why

- **Order details**: name, phone, email, delivery address (when you choose delivery) and a note, if you write one. We use them to prepare and deliver the order, to contact you about it, and to keep a record of the sale as the law requires.
- **Terms acceptance**: the time you accepted, and the versions of the Terms and the Privacy Policy you accepted, as proof of agreement.
- **Order language**: so we can send you emails in the language you chose.
- **Marketing emails**: only if you tick the box at checkout. We keep the tick and the date of consent (and the date of withdrawal, if you withdraw) as proof under the Communications Law. You can stop marketing emails at any time.
- **Messages through the contact form**: name, email and the message, so we can reply. The message is erased from the system as soon as it has been sent to us.
- **Browsing data**: we count page visits without identifying you: no IP address, cookie or other identifier is stored (see the [Cookie Policy](/en/pages/cookies)). An IP address is kept only as a one-way hash with a key that changes daily, to protect against abuse, for 24 hours at most.
- **Payment details**: payment is made directly to the seller, outside the site (bank transfer, Bit, PayBox or cash on pickup). The site does not collect payment details.

## Whether you must provide the data

There is no legal obligation to provide data, but without the order details we cannot prepare and deliver an order. Agreeing to marketing emails is always optional.

## How long we keep it

- **Orders**: customer details are kept for up to 7 years from the order date because of record-keeping and tax duties, and are then erased (we keep only amounts and items for accounting). You can ask for earlier erasure, as described below.
- **Unfinished payments**: the details entered at checkout are deleted within 48 hours, or up to 24 hours later if the payment provider cannot be reached for the final status check (so up to 72 hours), or after 30 days if the payment amount did not match.
- **System emails**: the email content is erased right after sending, and the record is deleted after 30 days.
- **Cookie choice** (only if optional cookies are ever turned on): two years.
- **Backups**: for recovery after a failure, encrypted copies are kept for up to 12 months. Erasure or anonymisation reaches backups only as the old copies expire.
- **Provider logs**: our hosting provider keeps IP addresses and request paths for a limited period for operations and security.

## Who receives the data

We use the following providers. Each receives only the data needed for its service:

- **Render**: hosting of the site and the database (Europe).
- **Cloudflare**: the site's DNS, storage and delivery of product images, and encrypted backups (European region). When images load, the provider sees visitors' IP addresses. [to be completed: check Render's sub-processor list to see whether Cloudflare also carries the site's traffic.]

We do not sell data and do not use it for third-party advertising.

## Transfers abroad

Some providers are located or operate outside Israel. The main providers store data in Europe, which Israel recognises as providing an adequate level of protection. We pass data only to providers that are bound to protect it, and only for the service.

## Your rights

Under the Privacy Protection Law you may:

- ask to see the data we hold about you and get a copy;
- ask us to correct wrong data;
- ask us to erase your data, as far as there is no legal duty to keep it (customer details in orders are erased, while amounts and items are kept for tax purposes);
- stop receiving marketing emails at any time.

To use a right, contact us through the [Contact](/en/contact) page or at the email at the bottom of the site. We will ask you to confirm that the request comes from you (for example through the email or phone number on the order) and will answer as soon as we can.

## Complaints to the Privacy Protection Authority

If you believe we have harmed your privacy, you can contact us, and you can also file a complaint with the Privacy Protection Authority at the Ministry of Justice.

## Customers from the European Union

If you order from the European Union, the GDPR also applies to you. The legal bases for processing are performing the order (contract), meeting legal duties (records and tax) and consent (for marketing emails and optional cookies). In addition to the rights above you may object to processing, restrict it and have your data transferred to another party. You can complain to the data protection authority in your country.
