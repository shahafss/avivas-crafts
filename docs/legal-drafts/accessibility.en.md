# Accessibility Statement

**Draft — requires legal review.** This text is not final and has not yet been checked by a professional.

We want everyone to be able to order from the site, including people with disabilities.

## Standard

The site was built to meet the Israeli Standard IS 5568, which is based on the WCAG 2.1 accessibility guidelines at level AA.

## What we did

- the site works in Hebrew right to left and in English left to right, and the language is marked on every page;
- the whole site can be used with a keyboard, and the keyboard position is clearly visible;
- every image and button has alternative text or an accessible name;
- colours have enough contrast, and information is not conveyed by colour alone;
- touch targets on a phone are large enough;
- forms show clear error messages, and screen readers announce them.
