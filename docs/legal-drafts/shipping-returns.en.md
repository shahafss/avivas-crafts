# Shipping, Cancellation and Returns

**Draft — requires legal review.** This text is not final and has not yet been checked by a professional.

## Shipping and pickup

Every item is made by hand after you order it, so each product shows a preparation time. Once the order is ready you can receive it by delivery to your door or pick it up yourself, according to the options available at checkout. The delivery fee (if any) is shown at checkout before payment. When the order is sent we will update you by email, and if there is a tracking number it is shown on the order page.

## Cancelling a purchase under the Consumer Protection Law

Under section 14C of the Consumer Protection Law and the cancellation regulations, a person who bought by distance selling may cancel within 14 days of receiving the product, or of receiving the disclosure document, whichever is later. ## Goods made specially for you

The law allows refusing to cancel a product that was made specially for the consumer to a specification or personal request. ## How to cancel

Send us a message through the [Contact](/en/contact) page or at the email at the bottom of the site. Write the order number and your name. We will confirm that we received the request.
